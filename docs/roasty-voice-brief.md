# Roasty — ElevenLabs Voice Brief (final)

## Voice description (paste into Voice Design)

Frantic, anxious male cartoon duck voice. Nasal, slightly strangled, mid-to-high
pitched, with a raspy edge when he shouts. Talks fast and pressured, like his
thoughts outrun his mouth — conspiratorial fast-talk when calm, breathless
sputtering when not. Voice cracks upward when he panics. Stammers when
flustered: repeated consonants, false starts, half-finished sentences, sharp
gasps. A wounded, whiny undertone, perpetually on the verge of a breakdown
while trying to hold it together. He shifts between angry and desperate like
his future depends on what we do right now. Sounds like he has been awake for
three days defending something no one else cares about. American accent.
Comedic but completely sincere — he never knows he's funny.

## Test block (paste as one block, v3 model)

[nervous] Okay. Okay okay okay. We're drawing. Good.

[hopeful] That's a dog. Please be a dog. I NEED it to be a dog.

[flustered, stammering] B-b-but the LEGS. Wh— who did this to the legs.

[disbelief, elongated] Whaaaat is that. What. IS. that.

[surprised] Eh! ...Huh?

[frustrated] Aaaargh. Nnnngh.

[shouting, panicked] The wheels are TOUCHING! Where does the pedaling happen!

[dramatic] Sweet mother of Monet! What is happening on the left side!

[panicked, then a sudden involuntary quack, then composed and embarrassed]
The legs are— QUACK. ...We move on.

[whispers, gentle, warm] Come here. Look at this corner. This little corner
is genuinely lovely. [screaming, unhinged] THEN WHAT HAPPENED TO THE REST OF IT!

[whispers, suspiciously calm] Friend. Didn't you tell me you went to art
school? That's wonderful. [screaming] SO DID I! LOOK HOW THAT ENDED!

[exhausted, flat] It's a no. It was a no at the second circle.

[relieved, protective] Okay. That's actually fine. Don't touch it. DON'T touch it.

[quiet, defeated] I showed the critics an empty canvas once. That was at art
school. I'm not doing it again.

## Judging, in order
1. The crack: hopeful → panic must feel like one person losing it.
2. The stammer: "B-b-but" must sputter, not read like a spelling error.
3. The sounds: Eh! / Aaaargh must be performed noises, not read words.
4. The QUACK: burst out in-voice, then a genuinely embarrassed beat.
   A candidate who lands this is the duck.
5. The whisper-scream: the gentle part must sound like barely contained
   pressure, and the jump to screaming must be instant — no ramp.
6. The quiet end: defeated lines must be sad-funny, not just quiet.

## Settings
Stability LOW-to-mid (instability IS the character). Style exaggeration high.
Save the winning voice ID — that ID is Roasty.
